👁️ Invisible Screen-Sharing & Overlay Guide

Follow these steps to keep MeetMind floating on your screen while staying 100% invisible to interviewers on Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet.

1
Click "📌 Pop Out HUD (Always On Top)"
Opens an ultra-lightweight floating teleprompter window that stays Always On Top of Teams, Zoom, VS Code, and browser tabs. Drag it right below your laptop webcam so you look directly into the camera while reading answers.
2
When Sharing Screen: Select "WINDOW" or "TAB" (Never "Entire Screen")
When the interviewer asks you to share your screen:
• Teams: Click Share → Choose Window (select your Code Editor or Browser test).
• Zoom: Click Share Screen → Select your specific browser/IDE window.
• Google Meet: Click Present now → Select "A window".
Why it works: Meeting apps encode only the selected application window. The floating PiP HUD is an independent window surface, so it is 100% excluded and completely invisible to everyone on the call!
3
Ghost Mode (Transparency) & Focus Presidency
Inside the HUD, use the Opacity dropdown (e.g. 75% or 60%) to make it semi-transparent so you can see your meeting or code right through it. You can click and type directly inside Teams, HackerRank, or VS Code — the meeting retains full focus while the HUD floats on top.
★
Advanced: Forced "Entire Screen" Sharing
If an interviewer insists you share your Entire Desktop, open the project folder and double-click stealth-mode.bat.
This launches the Native Stealth HUD with Windows OS-level WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE, making it 100% invisible on any desktop capture or screen share!
🔊
Capturing the Interviewer's Voice (Teams / Zoom / Meet)
Standard laptop microphones only hear your voice and cancel out laptop speakers. To hear the interviewer:
1. Click "🔊 Capture Teams Audio".
2. In the Chrome prompt, select "Entire Screen".
3. Make sure the toggle "Also share system audio" is checked at the bottom!
4. Click Share. MeetMind now intercepts the clean digital audio from Teams (even with headphones!) and feeds it directly to Gemini for instant question detection & answering.
